## Goal

You can tell a literal from an identifier, say which character sequences Python allows as a name, avoid the reserved words, and name multi word variables in a conventional way.

In the lesson: What is legal is distinct from what is conventional, or good practice, or recommended. Meaningful names for variables are important for the humans who are looking at programs, understanding them, and revising them. That sometimes means you would like to use a name that is more than one word long, like price at opening, but blanks are illegal. One poor option is just leaving out the blanks. Then it may be hard to figure out where the words split. Two practical options are on screen. The first is underscore separated: putting underscores, which are legal, in place of the blanks. The second is camel case: omitting spaces and using all lowercase, except capitalising all words after the first. Use the choice that fits your taste, or the taste or convention of the people you are working with.
