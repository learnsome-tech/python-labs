### Predict, then try

1. Write down your prediction for each of the six lines before you run any of them.
2. Predict twenty three floor divide five, twenty three remainder five, twenty remainder five.
3. Then predict six floor divide eight, six remainder eight, and six divided by eight.
4. Check all six at the shell, and work out why any prediction you got wrong was wrong.

> **Hint:** Two of the six use the same pair of numbers with three different operators. Compare those answers.

## Check yourself

- Which division operator always returns a float, even for six divided by three?

- Why does the remainder one test for odd numbers fail in Java for negative numbers?
