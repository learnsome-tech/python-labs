## Goal

You can build conditions with and, or and not, read their precedence and short circuit behaviour, and return a Boolean expression directly instead of wrapping it in an if else.

In the lesson: Two lines instead of four, and it reads like the sentence you would say out loud. The word or makes a compound condition that is true if at least one of its parts is true, and false only when both are false. Be careful here. English also uses or to mean exactly one alternative, as in tea or coffee. Python's or is never exclusive: if both parts are true, the whole thing is true. When you translate a problem stated in English, check which sense the problem meant.
