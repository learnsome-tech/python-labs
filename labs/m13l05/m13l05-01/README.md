## Goal

You can build conditions with and, or and not, read their precedence and short circuit behaviour, and return a Boolean expression directly instead of wrapping it in an if else.

In the lesson: To be eligible to graduate from Loyola University Chicago you must have one hundred and twenty credits and a grade point average of at least two. That English sentence has an and in it, and it translates straight into Python. On screen is a compound condition: two comparisons joined by the word and. The whole thing is itself a condition, so it has a Boolean value like any other. It is true when both parts are true, and false as soon as at least one part is false.
