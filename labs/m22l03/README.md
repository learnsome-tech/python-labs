## Check yourself

- What are the naming conventions for a function, a class and a constant?
- What is the difference between a formatter and a linter?
- Why did the formatter leave the unused import in place?
- Which of the three things the linter reported would crash the program, and why?
- What belongs in the first line of a docstring, and what does not belong anywhere in it?
