## Goal

You can find any graphics method for yourself in the reference, by knowing whether it belongs to every graphics object, to one particular type, or to the window, and you know the additions this tutorial makes to Zelle's package.

In the lesson: The third addition is for debugging. Every graphical type can be converted to a string, or printed, and it produces a descriptive string. Make a point, print it, and you get its type and its coordinates back. A line reports both of its ends the same way, each as a printed point. Be clear about the limit: it shows position only, so colour, width and whether it is drawn are all invisible here. Even so, when a shape has not appeared and you cannot see why, printing its position is often the fastest way to find it off the screen.
