## Goal

You can find any graphics method for yourself in the reference, by knowing whether it belongs to every graphics object, to one particular type, or to the window, and you know the additions this tutorial makes to Zelle's package.

In the lesson: Now the specialised methods, gathered under each type's own heading. You can ask a point for its horizontal and vertical coordinates, a circle for its radius and its centre, and a rectangle or an oval for its two defining corners. A polygon will hand back its list of points, and a line can be given an arrowhead on either end, or both. Text has by far the largest set of extras, all about appearance: replacing the words, reading them back, changing the size, the style, the typeface and the colour. The triangle program used several of these.
