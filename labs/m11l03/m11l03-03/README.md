## Goal

You can find any graphics method for yourself in the reference, by knowing whether it belongs to every graphics object, to one particular type, or to the window, and you know the additions this tutorial makes to Zelle's package.

In the lesson: Here is the whole cast, with the arguments each constructor expects. Look at the shapes of these calls rather than memorising them. Almost all of them want point objects, so a plain pair of numbers in round brackets will not do. The oval and the rectangle are described by two opposite corners of a box. The circle is the odd one out, taking a centre and a radius. The polygon takes a whole list of points, so it copes with any number of vertices. Text and entry are anchored at a point, and for text that point is the centre of the writing.
