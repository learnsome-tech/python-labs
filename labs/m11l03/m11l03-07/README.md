## Goal

You can find any graphics method for yourself in the reference, by knowing whether it belongs to every graphics object, to one particular type, or to the window, and you know the additions this tutorial makes to Zelle's package.

In the lesson: The version of the package that ships with this tutorial has a few elaborations of its own, and those will not be on Zelle's reference page. The first you have met already. When you create a window, the vertical coordinate increases going down the screen. Call the y up method straight after creating the window and the orientation reverses to the one you know from mathematics. Every coordinate you give afterwards is read in the new system. That is why the sample programs all call it on the line after they make the window.
