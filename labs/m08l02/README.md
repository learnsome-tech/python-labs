### Floating Point Exercise

1. Write a program, discount.py, prompting for an original price and a discount percentage.
2. Print the new price to the nearest penny, rounded rather than chopped.
3. Price two point eight nine with twenty percent off gives two point three one.
4. Price point six five with twenty five percent off gives point four nine.

> **Hint:** Write the general calculation following the pattern of the two worked examples, and remember that input hands you a string.

## Check yourself

- Why does five times two to the third give forty rather than one thousand?

- Why does point one printed to twenty places not end in zeros?
