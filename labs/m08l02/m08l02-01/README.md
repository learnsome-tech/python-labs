## Goal

You can raise numbers to powers, take square roots with a fractional exponent or with the math module, and round a float for display with the format function or a format string, without changing the value itself.

In the lesson: Exponentiation is finding powers. In mathematical notation you write three, then a small raised four, to mean three multiplied by itself four times. Python has no raised typography, so it uses two star characters before the power instead. Both lines on screen are worth trying in the shell, and the second is a trap. If you expected one thousand, remember that exponentiation has even higher precedence than multiplication and division. The power is worked out first: two to the third is eight, and five times eight is forty. The operator also groups from the right rather than the left, which is the one place Python arithmetic departs from the left to right habit you learned earlier.
