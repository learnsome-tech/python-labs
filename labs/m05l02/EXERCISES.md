## Exercise 1: Exercise for Addition

1. Write add3.py, asking for three numbers.
2. List all three numbers and their sum in one sentence.
3. Use a format similar to addition4.py, shown above.

> **Hint**: Three inputs, each converted with int; then one named sum; then one print with sep set to the empty string.


## Exercise 2: Exercise for Quotients

1. Write quotient.py, prompting for two integers.
2. Print them in a sentence with an integer division problem, in this form:
3. The quotient of 14 and 3 is 4 with a remainder of 2

> **Hint**: Review the section on division and remainders if you have forgotten the integer division or remainder operator.
