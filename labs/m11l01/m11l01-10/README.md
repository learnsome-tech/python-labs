## Goal

You can open a graphics window, construct points, circles, lines and rectangles, colour them, draw them, move them and close the window, and you know why the vertical axis points the wrong way.

In the lesson: The opening lines are off the top of the screen now, but the shapes are all still there. Objects can move after they have been drawn, which is what makes animation possible later. The move method takes two numbers: the amount to shift the x coordinate, and the amount to shift the y coordinate. Before you run this line, see if you can predict the result. Did you remember which way the vertical axis points? Because the coordinate system increases downward by default, adding forty moves the segment down the screen, not up.
