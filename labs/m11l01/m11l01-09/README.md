## Goal

You can open a graphics window, construct points, circles, lines and rectangles, colour them, draw them, move them and close the window, and you know why the vertical axis points the wrong way.

In the lesson: A rectangle is specified by two points as well, and they must be diagonally opposite corners. Here the first corner is given directly and the second is the point we made at the start. In this simple system a rectangle is always upright: its sides are horizontal and vertical, and there is no way to tilt one. For any other straight sided shape you use a polygon, which takes a whole list of vertices, and the next module puts that to work. Draw the rectangle and it appears on top, because the most recent thing drawn sits over everything else.
