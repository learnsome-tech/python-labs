## Goal

You can open a graphics window, construct points, circles, lines and rectangles, colour them, draw them, move them and close the window, and you know why the vertical axis points the wrong way.

In the lesson: The window has a Cartesian coordinate system, measured in pixels. The first coordinate is horizontal, counted from the left edge rightwards, so one hundred is about half way across a window two hundred pixels wide. The second coordinate is the one that surprises people. By default it increases going down from the top, not up as you expect from geometry class. So fifty out of two hundred is about a quarter of the way down from the top. Every position you work out has to respect that. In the next module we meet a method that flips the vertical axis back.
